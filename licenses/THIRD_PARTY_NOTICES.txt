Third-party software in the Rayzr editor and in games exported with it
====================================================================

Linked into the editor and the game runners: raylib (with GLFW, miniaudio,
stb, rlgl), Lua, Box2D, Odin's core library. The editor also: Dear ImGui,
cmark. Shipped with the editor: lua-language-server, Open Sans, Font Awesome.
